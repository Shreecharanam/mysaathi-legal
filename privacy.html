Privacy Policy

MySaathi — centre management app

Effective: 31 October 2026 Last updated: 31 October 2026 Applies to: MySaathi Android app

This Privacy Policy explains what information the MySaathi mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, how it is stored, and the choices you have. MySaathi is operated by Mukesh Sharma, India.

In short: MySaathi is a record-keeping app for coaching centres, gyms, clinics and shops. Centres use it to manage students, attendance, fees and tests. Parents and students can use it free of charge to view their own child's attendance, fees and marks when their centre gives them access. Your data is stored on your phone and in your own secure cloud account. We show advertisements (see section 5), we do not sell your data, and we do not collect your location, contacts or photos.

1. Who we are

MySaathi is a software application developed and operated by Mukesh Sharma. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us using the details in section 13.

2. Information we collect

a) Account information — when you sign in with Google or create an account:

  • Your name and email address
  • A unique account identifier created by our authentication provider

b) Centre profile information — that a centre enters itself:

  • Centre name and business type (coaching, gym, shop, other)
  • Optional: UPI ID, centre phone number, GSTIN, address

c) Records a centre creates inside the App — about the people at that centre:

CategoryExamples
PeopleStudent/member/customer name, phone number, parent or guardian phone number, class, batch, join date
AttendanceDates on which a person was marked present
FeesFee amount, fee period, amounts paid, pending amounts
TestsTest name, batch, maximum marks, marks obtained
Notes and billsShort notes added by the centre, and bills it creates

d) Information collected automatically by advertising:

  • A device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • General device and app information used to serve ads (device model, operating system version, approximate region, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (an ad was shown, tapped or closed)

See section 5 for details about advertising.

e) Information we do NOT collect:

  • Your precise location (GPS)
  • Your device contacts, SMS or call logs
  • Photos, camera or microphone content
  • Any data from other apps on your device

3. How your information is stored

  • On your device — the App keeps a local copy of your data so it works offline.
  • In the cloud — centre records are also saved to Google Cloud Firestore (part of Firebase), linked to the centre's signed-in account, so records can be restored if the phone is changed or reset.

Cloud data is stored under the centre's account. One centre cannot see another centre's data. A parent can see a student's records only when that student's centre gives them access (section 6).

4. Third-party services

We use the following services, which process data on our behalf:

  • Google Sign-In — to let a centre sign in with its Google account
  • Firebase Authentication — to manage sign-in and account security
  • Google Cloud Firestore — to store and sync centre records
  • Google AdMob — to display advertisements

These services are provided by Google. Their handling of data is governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not share centre records with any other third party.

5. Advertising

The App is free for parents and students, and is supported by advertisements shown through Google AdMob. Advertisements are not shown on a centre's paid management screens.

  • Google and its partners may use the device advertising identifier to show ads, including personalised ads based on your interests.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, at any time in your Android settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Opting out of personalised ads does not stop ads from being shown; it means the ads are less relevant to you.
  • We do not give your name, phone number, email or centre records to advertisers. Ad requests do not include that information.

For more information about how Google uses data in advertising, see Google's Privacy & Terms at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Parent and guardian access

A centre may give a parent or guardian an access code for their child. With that code, a parent can open the App and view, read-only:

  • their child's attendance record and attendance percentage
  • fees paid and fees pending
  • their child's batch or class
  • marks obtained in tests

A parent cannot change any of this information, and cannot see any other student's records or the centre's other data. The centre controls whether access is given, and can withdraw it at any time. Access codes are issued by the centre, not by us.

7. Why we use your information

  • To provide the App's features: student records, batches, attendance, fees, tests, reports
  • To let a parent or guardian view their own child's progress when the centre allows it
  • To keep data safe and restore it when the App is reinstalled
  • To show advertisements that keep the App free for parents and students
  • To respond to support requests

We do not use centre records for profiling or automated decision-making.

8. Sharing and disclosure

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share centre records with advertisers or data brokers.
  • When you tap "Send on WhatsApp" or "Pay by UPI", the App opens those apps with content that you chose to send. That content then leaves the App and is handled by those apps under their own policies.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request.

9. Children's information

The App does not create accounts for students. Centres, which are run by adults, enter and manage student records.

Because a centre may enter records about students under 18 years of age, the centre is responsible for that data and must have the consent of the student's parent or guardian before entering it and before giving parent access. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Centre records stay in the centre's account until the centre deletes them.
  • Delete centre data: in the App, Settings → Delete centre data.
  • Delete account: in the App, Settings → Delete account. This removes the centre's records from the device and from the cloud.
  • You may also email us at mysaathiapp@gmail.com to request deletion, and we will act on it.

11. Security

Data sent between the App and our cloud services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to a centre's cloud records requires signing in to that centre's account. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • View and correct your data inside the App
  • Export records (Settings → Export / Backup)
  • Delete your data or your account
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings
  • Contact us with any question or complaint about your data
