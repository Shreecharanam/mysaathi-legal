MySaathi

Centre management app — attendance, fees, marks

Get the app

Download for Android Google Play — coming soon

If the download does not start, ask your centre to share the MySaathi app file with you.

Have an access code?

  1. Install the MySaathi app on your phone.
  2. Open the app. On the first screen, tap "Parent? View your child record".
  3. Enter the access code your centre sent you — for example K7QP4M2X.
  4. Tap View record. You will see attendance, fees and test marks.

The code is personal. Anyone who has it can view that student's record, so please do not share it publicly.

For centres

MySaathi helps coaching centres, gyms, clinics and shops keep student records, mark daily attendance, track fees and dues, record test marks and send reports to parents. One account manages one centre.

For pricing and activation, contact mysaathiapp@gmail.com.

The app is free for parents and students. Centres pay a yearly subscription for the management features.
