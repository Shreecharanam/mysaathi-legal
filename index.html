Privacy Policy

MySaathi — centre management app

Effective: 31 October 2026 Last updated: 31 October 2026 Applies to: MySaathi Android app

This Privacy Policy explains what information the MySaathi mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, how it is stored, and the choices you have. MySaathi is operated by Mukesh Sharma, India.

In short: MySaathi is a record-keeping app for coaching centres, gyms, clinics and shops. Your data is stored on your phone and in your own secure cloud account so it can be restored if you reinstall the App. We do not sell your data, we do not show advertisements, and we do not collect your location, contacts or photos.

1. Who we are

MySaathi is a software application developed and operated by Mukesh Sharma. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us using the details in section 11.

2. Information we collect

a) Account information — when you sign in with Google or create an account:

  • Your name and email address
  • A unique account identifier created by our authentication provider

b) Centre profile information — that you enter yourself:

  • Centre name and business type (coaching, gym, shop, other)
  • Optional: UPI ID, centre phone number, GSTIN, address

c) Records you create inside the App — entered by you, about the people at your centre:

CategoryExamples
PeopleStudent/member/customer name, phone number, parent or guardian phone number, class, batch, join date
AttendanceDates on which a person was marked present
FeesFee amount, fee period, amounts paid, pending amounts
TestsTest name, batch, maximum marks, marks obtained
Notes and billsShort notes you add, and bills you create

d) Information we do NOT collect:

  • Your location (GPS)
  • Your device contacts, SMS or call logs
  • Photos, camera or microphone content
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any data from other apps on your device

3. How your information is stored

  • On your device — the App keeps a local copy of your data so it works offline.
  • In the cloud — your data is also saved to Google Cloud Firestore (part of Firebase), linked to your signed-in account, so that your records can be restored if you change or reset your phone.

Cloud data is stored under your account. Other users of the App cannot see your data, and you cannot see theirs.

4. Third-party services

We use the following services, which process data on our behalf:

  • Google Sign-In — to let you sign in with your Google account
  • Firebase Authentication — to manage sign-in and account security
  • Google Cloud Firestore — to store and sync your records

These services are provided by Google. Their handling of data is governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not share your records with any other third party.

5. Why we use your information

  • To provide the App's features: student records, batches, attendance, fees, tests, reports
  • To keep your data safe and restore it when you reinstall the App
  • To let you share a report with a parent or guardian when you choose to
  • To respond to your support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making.

6. Sharing and disclosure

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • When you tap "Send on WhatsApp" or "Pay by UPI", the App opens those apps with content that you chose to send. That content then leaves the App and is handled by those apps under their own policies.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request.

7. Children's information

MySaathi is designed for centre owners and staff, who are adults. The App does not create accounts for students.

However, a centre may enter records about students who are under 18 years of age (for example, a name, class and attendance). In that case, the centre is the one responsible for that data, and the centre must have the consent of the student's parent or guardian before entering it. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays in your account until you delete it.
  • Delete centre data: in the App, open Settings → Delete centre data.
  • Delete your account: in the App, open Settings → Delete account. This removes your centre records from your device and from the cloud.
  • You may also email us at mysaathiapp@gmail.com to request deletion, and we will act on it.

9. Security

Data sent between the App and our cloud services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to your cloud records requires signing in to your account. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • View and correct your data inside the App
  • Export your records (Settings → Export / Backup)
  • Delete your data or your account
  • Contact us with any question or complaint about your data
